TrackFormer (MSc dissertation)

Transformer for particle track fitting on TrackML, a roughly 5 TB detector data set with noise hits. Found that quantile loss is far more robust than MSE on noisy, high volume data.

  • PyTorch Lightning
  • Transformers
  • large data

Leakage aware medical ML benchmark

Dementia classification on OASIS-1, comparing tabular baselines with an MRI CNN. Subject level splits prevent leakage; calibration and error analysis show tabular models reach about 0.80 ROC AUC.

  • scikit-learn
  • calibration
  • model validation
